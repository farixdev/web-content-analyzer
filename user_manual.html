1. What Is This Tool & What Does It Do?

The Mindcob Web Content Analyzer is an automated SEO intelligence tool. In plain English, it answers one simple question:

"When people search Google for a specific topic, what exact words, phrases, and topics are the top-ranking competitors using across their entire websites?"

Instead of guessing what to write in an article, blog post, or service landing page, this tool scans the actual websites currently ranking on Google. It reads 20 to 40+ internal pages for each competitor (not just their homepage), counts every word, and presents you with a clear roadmap of:

  • Which keywords and phrases are shared by all competitors (core topics you must cover).
  • How often each competitor uses those keywords (exact counts and percentage densities).
  • Every single webpage that was crawled, with total word counts and direct links.
  • Live Google autocomplete suggestions that real people are searching for.
  • One-click publication-ready Microsoft Word reports (.docx) with embedded charts and spreadsheets (.csv) to hand directly to writers or clients.
100% Free Organic Engine: You do NOT need any paid API keys (like Serper, Semrush, or Ahrefs). The built-in organic engine queries Google and crawls websites directly at zero cost.

2. Quick Start: How to Run an Analysis in 3 Steps

  1. Type Your Topic or Keyword: In the Search Query box, enter the topic you want to rank for (e.g., pos system montreal or business voip for law firms).
  2. Pick Your Target Country: Select where your target audience lives (e.g. United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia). Google search results change by country; the tool queries Google from that specific location.
  3. Click "Run Deep Analysis": The crawler starts immediately. You will see a live progress bar and a log console showing every page being scraped in real time.

Within 30 to 60 seconds, the crawler finishes and reveals all charts, tables, and download buttons.

4. Understanding the Results: What the Tabs & Charts Show

Once the scan completes, you get four interactive views:

Tab 1: Keyword Dominance Matrix (The Core Feature)

This is the most powerful part of the tool. It shows which keywords are used by multiple competitors at the same time.

  • The Bar Chart: Shows the top 15 most frequent shared keywords across all competitors at a glance.
  • The Matrix Table: Lists every shared keyword, its Competitor Overlap (e.g., "4 / 5 sites (80%)"), the total number of times it was written, and exactly how many times each competitor used it.
  • Why this matters for your content: If a keyword is used by 5 out of 5 competitors, Google expects to see that topic in any page that wants to rank on page 1.
  • Search Filter Box: You can type any word into the table filter box to immediately see if competitors mentioned it and how many times.

Tab 2: Competitor Deep Dive

Allows you to inspect any competitor individually:

  • Domain Pills: Click on any competitor button (e.g., competitor1.com) to view their specific statistics.
  • Top 20 Keywords Chart & Table: Shows their exact highest-frequency keywords, how many times they appear, and their density percentage.
  • Crawled Pages Directory: A complete list of all 20–40 pages scraped from that competitor, their page titles, word counts, and a direct link to visit the original page.

Tab 3: Google Autocomplete Suggestions

This tab displays real-time search queries scraped directly from Google's live autocomplete dropdown for your keyword.

  • These chips represent related questions and search variations that real users are actively typing into Google.
  • 1-Click Re-Analysis: Clicking on any suggestion chip automatically puts it into the search box and lets you run a fresh analysis for that sub-topic.

Tab 4: Organic SERP Results

Shows the original Google search engine results page as it appeared:

  • Every ranked listing (#1, #2, #3, etc.) with title, domain, and meta snippet.
  • Shows which websites were selected as business competitors and which links were automatically filtered out (e.g. Reddit forums, social media, or ads).

6. Practical Tips: How to Write an Article Using This Data

Here is the exact 4-step workflow to turn this data into a top-ranking article or service page:

  1. Identify the "Must-Have" Topics (100% Overlap): Look at the Keyword Dominance Matrix table. Any keyword that has 4/5 or 5/5 competitor presence is a core requirement. Create an H2 or H3 heading in your outline covering that topic.
  2. Match the Average Word Count: Check the Total Words Parsed KPI. Divide that by the number of pages to see the average page length. If competitors average 1,500 words on their top pages, aim for 1,600–1,800 words of higher-quality content.
  3. Use 2–3 Word Phrases for Natural Flow: Set the N-Gram filter to Phrases (2-3 words). Work these phrases naturally into your body text and bullet points instead of stuffing single words repeatedly.
  4. Answer Google Suggestions: Grab the top 3–5 questions from the Google Suggestions tab and turn them into an FAQ section at the bottom of your page.
